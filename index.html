Featured, DTACT

Virtual Data Lake MVP

The problem

Data providers require heavy ETL pipelines and complex implementations, plus vendor lock-in, and most options are US-based, leaving the EU market with very few alternatives.

The solution

An EU-made, virtual-first data lake: query data where it lives, skip the heavy pipelines and storage, apply permissions, and expose it through an API that tools like Claude Code, Postgres and OData clients speak natively. Scoped, built and shipped in roughly two months; adopted immediately as DTACT's first enterprise deployment.

The geophysics pilot

The problem

Fugro's ocean-survey geophysicists turn raw magnetometer and positioning data into target picks, flagging unexploded ordnance, cables and other seabed hazards, by manually applying filters using an expensive, vendor-locked third-party tool.

We built a pilot that ingests standard data inputs, lets users configure it per project, and outputs the files their geophysics software expects, automating a specialist manual workflow and cutting Fugro's dependency on that vendor.

03 - Tesla

AXI: the D365 accounting engine

The problem

Tesla's EMEA team supports 40+ legal entities across 25+ jurisdictions, each with its own compliance requirements, local integrations and logic. The accounting engine also has to integrate with Tesla's internal tools, including the internal "Warp" system spanning logistics, delivery, billing, payments, supercharging and ordering, which demanded fully customized accounting logic. On top of that, new GDPR rules pushed for localizing the databases holding sensitive production data.

The solution

Led product efforts for the team that deployed AXI, an EU-based accounting engine that automates the bulk of transaction volume (70%+): tens of billions of dollars logged yearly, with 15M+ data entries processed daily. New rules, edge cases and reconciliation moved out of analysts' inboxes and into the engine: custom logic for wealth tax in Norway and Turkey, withholding tax handling, adjusting transaction granularity, and more.
